The Italian Republic

The Beauty of Italian Nature

Italy boasts some of Europe’s most diverse natural landscapes, ranging from the dramatic jagged limestone peaks of the Dolomites and the Alpine meadows of Gran Paradiso National Park to the terraced coastal cliffs of Cinque Terre and the sparkling waters of Lake Garda. Further south, the rolling cypress-lined hills of Tuscany blend with the geothermal thermal springs of Saturnia and the fiery, raw volcanic presence of Mount Etna in Sicily, creating a rich tapestry of mountains, lakes, and coastlines.
