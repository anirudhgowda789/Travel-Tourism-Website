About Abu Dhabi

Abu Dhabi is the capital city of the United Arab Emirates and the largest of its seven emirates. Sitting mostly on a Persian Gulf island with ties to the mainland, it grew from a pearl-diving and fishing hub into a major economic center after oil discoveries in the 1950s. Today, the city manages vast sovereign wealth and diversifies into finance, tourism, and clean energy. Visitors and residents see modern skyscrapers alongside landmarks like the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, the Louvre Abu Dhabi, and desert oases.
